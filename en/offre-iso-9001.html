Support proposal — quality management system (QMS) ISO 9001:2015

This offer outlines the usual assignment phases: diagnosis, action plan implementation, certification audit preparation and training modules. It is indicative; a tailored proposal can be prepared after reviewing your context.

1. Purpose of the offer

Implement a QMS meeting the requirements of ISO 9001:2015. AHF CONSULTING provides support through certification by an accredited third-party body, with a results-oriented approach and normative compliance.

2. Benefits of quality management

  • Customer satisfaction: better delivery on commitments and clearer expectations.
  • Fewer internal conflicts: clearer workflows, saving time and energy, better climate.
  • Streamlined methods: useful procedures supported by the manual and quality plan.
  • Financial performance: better control of lost sales and investments.
  • Activity steering: indicators for management to monitor operational performance.

3. Project objectives

  • Define and deploy a complete QMS, tailored to the company and ready for ISO 9001:2015 certification.
  • Support the organisation while the system is put into practice.
  • Train staff on quality concepts, standard requirements and quality approaches.
  • Support through to final certification.

4. Assignment flow (main phases)

The team implements the QMS on an agreed schedule. Below are the first two phases in detail; certification preparation and training follow.

Phase 1 — Quality diagnosis

Activities

  • Document audit: compare existing procedures to ISO 9001:2015, identify gaps, check process descriptions are complete.
  • Field audit: check real application of procedures and identify unmet requirements.
  • Action plan: roadmap to deploy the quality system.

Deliverables

  • Diagnosis report (with company profile / identity sheet).
  • Detailed action plan.

Phase 2 — Implementing the action plan

A. Preparation, awareness and training

  • Method: deployment strategy, steering committee and quality groups.
  • Processes: identify key processes (quality, engineering, production, HR, finance, etc.) and train managers.
  • Management & supervision: training on quality concepts and ISO 9001:2015 (including quality manager where applicable).

B. Operational deployment of the QMS

  • Documentation: develop and formalise QMS processes.
  • Technical training: writing procedures, work instructions and the quality manual.
  • Practical support: guidance during on-site rollout.

Deliverables

  • Implementation reports.
  • Training certificates.

Certification audit preparation

Typical activities before the certification body visit:

  • Support for internal verification of the management system.
  • Deployment of the internal audit process.
  • Deployment of management review.
  • Mock audit ahead of ISO 9001:2015 certification.
  • Help with the certification file and follow-up until the certificate is issued.

Deliverable: mock audit report.

QMS-related training

Topic Type Audience Duration Objective
ISO 9001:2015 interpretation Advanced Process owners 2 days Understand QMS requirements
Process management Advanced Process owners 2 days Define processes and write descriptive sheets
Internal quality audit (practical) Advanced Process owners 3 days Audit a QMS per ISO 19011:2018

Project execution

The assignment uses a methodology and a provisional schedule, adjustable during the mission according to your needs. Work is done in close cooperation with a company representative (e.g. quality manager). Consultants adapt to site pace and constraints.

  • Indicative duration: 2 to 4 months from contract signature (depending on scope and availability).
  • Client responsibilities: consultants’ transport, accommodation and meals, plus human and material resources needed for the mission.

Confidentiality

Accepting the proposal implies mutual confidentiality: AHF CONSULTING undertakes not to disclose information about the client; the client undertakes to use documents and methodological tools only for its own purposes.

Fee schedule (indicative)

Payment by phase, at the end of each stage:

40 % after quality diagnosis
40 % after QMS implementation
20 % upon confirmation of ISO 9001:2015 certification

Final percentages and terms are set out in the signed contract and commercial proposal.
