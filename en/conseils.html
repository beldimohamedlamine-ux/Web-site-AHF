Consulting formats

  • Targeted mission: focused support for one critical issue.
  • Monthly support: continuous guidance over 3, 6 or 12 months.
  • Executive coaching: strategic support for management decisions.
  • Team workshops: operational alignment and ownership on the field.

Expected deliverables

  • 90-day roadmap with clear priorities.
  • Risk and opportunity matrix linked to processes.
  • Detailed action plan with owners and milestones.
  • Monitoring dashboard with operational KPI.
  • Executive synthesis for steering committees.
