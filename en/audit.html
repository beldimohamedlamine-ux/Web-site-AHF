Audit

Our internal audits and compliance reviews help identify gaps early and improve overall QHSE performance.

Audit types

Targeted audit services aligned with your maturity level, obligations and certification timeline.

Internal audit

Assessment of system effectiveness and real implementation on site.

Pre-certification mock audit

Full simulation before the official certification audit to reduce major findings.

Supplier audit

Evaluation of critical suppliers on quality, safety and compliance practices.

Regulatory compliance audit

Gap review against applicable legal requirements and compliance priorities.

Our 5-step method

  1. Preparation: scope definition, standards, audit plan and schedule.
  2. Field audit: interviews, document review, observation and sampling.
  3. Reporting: structured findings, nonconformities and risk points.
  4. Action plan: prioritization by criticality with owners and deadlines.
  5. Follow-up: verification of closure and action effectiveness.

Deliverables

  • Completed audit report with objective evidence.
  • non-conformities report (minor/major) with risk ranking.
  • CAPA plan with timeline and responsibilities.
  • Management summary for decision-making.
  • Roadmap for upcoming audits and reviews.

Suggested KPI monitoring

Simple indicators to track progress and system robustness.

Finding closure rateShare of findings closed within agreed deadlines.
Average closure timeTime from finding detection to effective closure.
Recurrence rateFrequency of repeated findings by category.
Audit coverageShare of critical processes audited during the period.

Audit FAQ

What is an audit?

Systematic, independent and documented process for obtaining audit evidence and evaluating it objectively to determine the extent to which the audit criteria are fulfilled.

How long does an assignment take?

Depending on the scope, from one to several days. A schedule is set in advance to limit operational impact.

Do you work across several ISO standards?

Yes, including ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 22000/HACCP and other standards.
